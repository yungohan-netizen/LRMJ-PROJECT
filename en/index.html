A rare specialty · Brussels

Restoring
cast-iron balustrades

Few craftsmen still master the restoration of antique cast iron. Romeo brings your period balustrades, railings and decorative elements back to life: cleaning, specialised welding, replacement of missing parts, anti-corrosion treatment and period-accurate finishing.

  • ✓Cleaning & stripping of the cast iron
  • ✓Welding and replacement of missing parts
  • ✓Anti-corrosion treatment & painting
  • ✓Railings, balconies, brackets, mouldings

Official grant

Façade Restoration Grant

The City of Brussels reimburses 40 to 60% of façade restoration costs, including cast-iron balconies, railings and brackets, up to € 14,000 per file. Other municipalities run their own schemes: ask us for details.

40 % Façade ≤ 10 m max € 6 000
50 % Façade 10-20 m max € 10 000
60 % Façade > 20 m max € 14 000
