Custom steel windows and glass partitions

Letting light in without losing character: that's the whole art of a steel-framed glass partition. Workshop-style partitions, windows, winter gardens, designed and built for your space.

The slimness of steel, the strength of custom-made

What makes a steel-framed partition so appealing is the slimness of its profiles: uprights slimmer than any standard joinery can match, a grid pattern designed to your taste, an immediate sense of space. We build indoor glass partitions (between kitchen and living room, around a home office, at the top of a staircase), steel windows and winter gardens that extend the house outward.

Every piece starts with a precise survey of your opening and a design: pane proportions, solid or glazed base, an integrated door, a fanlight. The structure is then fabricated in our Schaerbeek workshop and installed by our own team.

Indoor or outdoor

Indoors, a glass partition divides without darkening: it separates functions while letting light travel through. Outdoors, windows and winter gardens get glazing suited to the use (laminated or double glazing depending on the project) and a powder-coated finish that protects the steel for the long run, in the colour of your choice.

The structures stay visually light but structurally solid — that's the nature of forged steel, and why these pieces last for generations.

One project, one point of contact

As with all our work, you deal directly with the craftsman who builds it: on-site visit, measurements, design, free quote within 48 hours, workshop fabrication and careful installation. See also our workshop and how we work.

Frequently asked questions

What does a steel glass partition cost?

It varies with the dimensions, the number of bays, whether it includes a door, and the type of glazing. The quote is free, detailed, and delivered within 48 hours of the visit.

What glazing do you use?

It depends on the use: laminated glass for indoor safety, double glazing for pieces facing outdoors. We recommend the right solution during the visit.

Can a door be built into the partition?

Yes, hinged or sliding, with the same slim profiles. It's designed to continue the grid pattern so the whole piece stays visually coherent.
