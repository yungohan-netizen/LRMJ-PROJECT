Privacy Policy

Personal data collected

Only the fields you voluntarily fill in on the contact form (name, email, phone, message, project type) are collected. They are used solely to get back to you about your request.

Retention

Your data is kept for as long as strictly necessary to process your request, then archived or deleted within 24 months at most.

Sharing with third parties

Your data is never sold, rented or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes. It is only processed by LRMJ Project and its form-submission provider.

Cookies used

  • Strictly necessary cookies: theme preference (light/dark), cookie banner confirmation. No tracking.
  • No third-party advertising cookies, no Facebook or Google Ads pixels.

The site uses anonymous, cookie-free visit statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics): no personal data, no identifier, no cross-site tracking.

You can delete local data (preferences) at any time from your browser settings.

Your rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@lrmj-project.be.

Supervisory authority

In the event of an unresolved dispute, you can contact the Belgian Data Protection Authority: autoriteprotectiondonnees.be.
