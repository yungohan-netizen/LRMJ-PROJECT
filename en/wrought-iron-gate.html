Wrought-iron gates and fencing

The entrance sets the tone for the whole property. We design and forge custom gates that combine presence, security and durability, across Brussels and Flemish Brabant.

A gate designed for your property

A wrought-iron gate is never a catalogue product here. Every project starts from your actual entrance: the width, the slope, the style of the façade, whether there's a wall or pillars. We then design a model to match, from clean contemporary lines to classic scrollwork, spearheads or a monogram.

Double-leaf hinged or sliding depending on the layout, a matching wicket gate, fencing in the same design to extend the whole: everything comes out of the same workshop, by the same hand.

Forged to last outdoors

A gate lives outside all year round. That's why the finish matters as much as the design: depending on exposure and your preferences, we protect the steel with hot-dip galvanising, powder coating (any RAL colour) or epoxy paint. These treatments give the piece decades of corrosion resistance, with minimal maintenance.

The structure is also designed from the outset to accept motorisation if you want it, now or later.

From visit to installation

We come to see your entrance, take measurements and understand your constraints. You receive a free, detailed quote within 48 hours. Fabrication happens in our Schaerbeek workshop, installation by our own team: anchoring, alignment, hinge adjustment and finishing. One point of contact from start to finish — that's how we work.

Frequently asked questions

What does a wrought-iron gate cost?

It depends on the dimensions, the design (plain or highly ornate), the opening type and the finish. Custom work means case-by-case pricing: the visit and the quote are free, and you get an answer within 48 hours.

Hinged or sliding, how do I choose?

A hinged gate is the classic choice when there's enough clearance to swing open. Sliding gates make sense when the driveway slopes, space is tight, or you want the entrance fully clear. We decide together on-site, based on your terrain.

Can a wrought-iron gate be motorised?

Yes. We design the structure to accept a motor suited to its weight and opening style, whether you install it right away or later on.

Do you also make matching fencing?

Of course, and it's even recommended: fencing, wicket gate and main gate in the same design give a cohesive entrance. Everything comes from the same workshop.
